Twisty Net

Privacy policy

Last updated November 8, 2026

In short

  • There are no accounts. Your progress stays on your device.
  • We run no servers and no analytics of our own, so nothing you do in the game is sent to us.
  • Reward videos come from Google AdMob, and only if you choose to watch one. Until then, the ad system never starts.
  • If you buy Remove Ads, the ad system never starts at all.

Who we are

Twisty Net is made by Logan Wood, an independent developer. In this policy, “we” means the developer. You can reach us at lwoodapps@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

The game keeps these on your iPhone or iPad only:

  • your unfinished puzzle, past results, statistics and best times;
  • your settings and chosen Theme;
  • your Tokens, Hints and Theme unlocks;
  • whether you own Remove Ads, with the purchase record Apple provides.

These are included in your device backups (iCloud or your computer), which Apple handles under its own privacy policy. We never receive them. Deleting the app erases them. A Remove Ads purchase can always be restored from the App Store.

Reward videos and Google AdMob

You can watch an optional video to earn Tokens or Hints. The videos come from Google AdMob. The ad software starts only after you tap Watch a video for the first time, and never if you own Remove Ads.

When the ad software runs, Google receives:

  • device and app identifiers, including your device’s advertising identifier, but only if you allow tracking (see below);
  • your approximate location, worked out from your IP address;
  • which ads you were shown and how you interacted with them;
  • crash, performance and other diagnostic data about the ads.

Google uses this to show ads, to measure them, to prevent fraud and, if you allow it, to tailor ads to you. Google handles this data under its own policies: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. We don’t receive this data about you. Google gives us only totals, such as how many ads were shown.

Ads are limited to content rated T (teen) or lower, and we block categories such as gambling, dating, alcohol, politics and religion.

Tracking and your choices

Before your first video, the game explains what happens next, then your device asks whether Google may use its advertising identifier. Either answer is fine, and the reward is the same.

  • If you allow it, ads may be personalized.
  • If you don’t, ads are not personalized, Google does not get the advertising identifier, and we ask Google not to use its own publisher identifier.

You can change this at any time in your device’s Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

In the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland and US states with privacy laws, Google may also show a consent or opt-out message before the first video. Once you’ve seen it, Settings → Privacy → Privacy options in the game lets you change your choice.

Declining never stops you from playing. Free Tokens and Hints refill every day either way.

We don’t sell your personal information. If you allow tracking, Google may use your advertising identifier to show personalized ads, which some US state laws call “sharing”. You can stop that by not allowing tracking, or through Privacy options where they’re offered.

Remove Ads

Remove Ads is a one-time purchase made through Apple. Apple handles the payment, and we never see your payment details. The game keeps Apple’s purchase record on your device so it knows you own Remove Ads. With Remove Ads, the ad software never starts and nothing is sent to Google.

Children

Twisty Net is a general-audience game. It is not directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Your rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. To erase the game’s data, delete the app. For data Google receives during videos, use Privacy options, your device’s tracking setting, or Google’s own tools at My Ad Center. If you have a question or request, email us and we’ll help.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and the date at the top. If a change affects what is shared, the game will say so too.
